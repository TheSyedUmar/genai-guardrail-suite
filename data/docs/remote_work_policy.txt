Hybrid schedule: Northwind follows a hybrid model. Employees must work from the office at least 3 days per week. Tuesday and Thursday are mandatory office days for all teams.

Fully remote requests: Fully remote work is allowed only with written approval from the employee's department head, reviewed every 6 months.

Home office allowance: Each employee receives a one-time home office allowance of 15,000 rupees for a chair, desk or monitor.

Working hours: Core collaboration hours are 11 AM to 4 PM IST. Employees must be reachable on chat during core hours.
