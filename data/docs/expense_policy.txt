Submitting expenses: Expense claims must be submitted in the finance portal within 30 days of the expense date, with a scanned receipt attached.

Travel meals: The daily meal allowance during business travel is 1,500 rupees per day within India.

Approval limits: Claims up to 10,000 rupees are approved by the reporting manager. Claims above 10,000 rupees also need approval from the finance team.

Reimbursement timeline: Approved expenses are reimbursed with the next monthly salary.
