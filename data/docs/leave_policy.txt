Annual leave: Every full-time employee at Northwind receives 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave is credited on January 1st.

Carry forward: Up to 5 unused annual leave days can be carried forward to the next year. Any extra unused days lapse on December 31st.

Sick leave: Employees get 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.

Parental leave: Birthing parents receive 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Non-birthing parents receive 4 weeks of paid parental leave.

Applying for leave: Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 7 days in advance, except for sick leave.
